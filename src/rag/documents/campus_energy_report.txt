Campus Energy Optimization Report

The college campus consists of five major facilities:

1. AI Lab
2. Library
3. Hostel
4. Administration Block
5. Classroom Block

Average energy usage:

AI Lab:
Highest electricity consumption because of GPUs and servers.

Library:
Moderate electricity usage.

Hostel:
High night-time electricity consumption.

Administration:
Low electricity usage.

Classrooms:
Peak usage between 9 AM and 4 PM.

Optimization Strategies

• Shift laboratory work after 6 PM during non-peak hours.
• Install rooftop solar panels.
• Replace fluorescent lights with LED lighting.
• Use motion sensors in classrooms.
• Schedule HVAC systems automatically.

Common Energy Anomalies

• Faulty AC units
• Lights left ON overnight
• Laboratory equipment running after office hours

Sustainability Goals

Reduce campus energy consumption by 15%.

Reduce carbon emissions by 10%.

Increase renewable energy usage to 30%.
